Send

How outbound mail is queued and drained, and how tens of thousands of messages are absorbed.

Outbound mail is submitted (HTTP, CLI, SMTP 587), stored in an outbound spool, and drained by a sender plugin at a controlled rate. The spool absorbs bursts: tens of thousands of messages are accepted quickly and sent at the pace remote servers allow.

  • Sizing. 10-50k messages at 75-100 KB is about 1-5 GB. max_spool_bytes caps it; at the limit, submitters get a retryable 4xx. Trunk is not meant for terabytes.
  • Bulk bodies are stored once, with a small per-recipient envelope pointing at them.
  • Drain. Per-destination-domain concurrency and rate limits, reused connections, retry with exponential backoff and jitter, and a bounce for permanent failures.
  • Sharding. Outbound work is sharded by destination-domain hash, so each domain's throttle is exact inside one worker.
  • Open: DSN format and the retry schedule.
